## State authority summary

Work identity/lifecycle belongs to the durable work contract; schedule definition and next-due
identity belong to scheduling; claim/lease/fence/attempt belong to execution; publication state
and ACK belong to publication; stored object identity belongs to storage; access result identity
belongs to access. Canonical registration evidence is owned by its designated registration record,
while the publication state `CANONICALLY_REGISTERED` is a verified projection of that evidence.
